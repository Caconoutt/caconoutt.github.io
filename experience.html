TeamWork - 22 Summer

I completed a four-week remote internship with Adviza, a British company that provides consultation services to students and parents. I was under the project Equity, Diversity, & Inclusion (E, D&I) supervised by Maninder Hayre.
I evaluated the charter of E, D&I by conducting research and proposing missions and visions that it can adopt to further improvement. In weekly meetings, I communicated and collaborated with a team of three to present our work to the supervisor. To assess the extent of E, D&I in the workplace, I designed a questionnaire to evaluate workers ’ awareness of E, D&I, and analysed the results. As final work, I proposed the annual Shine a Light - Make it Bright campaign consisting of monthly distinct events to enhance workers ’ engagement in E, D&I.

E-Mentorship at New College - 22 Summer

I interacted with and led a group of approximately 300 incoming students to develop a supportive New College community. To assist students in familiarizing themselves with the campus, I provided promotional content regarding New College students' life at residence and shared university resources that are academic- or life-related.
