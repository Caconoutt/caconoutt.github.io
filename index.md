## Job Experience
I worked as a software engineer intern in [Quanta Technology](https://quanta-technology.com/) from May, 2024 to August, 2025. I contributed as a full-stack developer in an Agile environment to develop a finite-state-control workflow portal, significantly reducing manual overhead and improving client operation accuracy.

--- 

## Research Experience
I worked as a research assitant supervised by Niv Dayan on the project minimal perfect hashing on [RocksDB](https://github.com/facebook/rocksdb). I designed, implemented, and benchmarked a minimal perfect hash index integrated within the SST data-block write and read pipelines to optimize index lookups and enhance read throughput.
