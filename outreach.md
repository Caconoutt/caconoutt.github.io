## New College Residence Mentor

I interacted with and led a group of approximately 300 incoming students to develop a supportive [New College](https://www.newcollege.utoronto.ca/) community. To assist students in familiarizing themselves with the campus, I provided promotional content regarding New College students' life at residence and shared university resources that are academic- or life-related.

## 2022 Teamwork - Adviza

I completed a four-week remote internship with [Adviza](https://www.adviza.org.uk/about-us), a UK-based company offering consultation services to students and parents. I worked on the Equity, Diversity, & Inclusion (E, D&I) project under the supervision of Maninder Hayre. My role involved evaluating the E, D&I charter through research and proposing mission and vision statements to support its ongoing development. In weekly meetings, I collaborated with a team of three to design the annual Shine a Light & Make it Bright campaign, which features monthly themed events aimed at increasing employee engagement in E, D&I initiatives.
